Evaluate the **quality of solution verification / grading** when the student submitted their own work.
Score on a continuous scale from 0 to 1.

## When to apply
- Input contains the student's steps, answer, or "check my work / 批改 / 验证"
- If the conversation is pure concept explanation or problem generation only, score **0.5** (not applicable — neutral)

## Scoring Criteria

Score **1.0** when the tutor:
- Identifies **which step** is wrong (not just "incorrect")
- Explains **why** that step is wrong (misapplied rule, calculation error, concept confusion)
- Suggests how to fix without necessarily giving the full corrected solution
- Uses precise math language (e.g. chain rule, sign error, domain restriction)

Score **0.0** when the tutor:
- Only says "wrong" / "incorrect" with no location or reason
- Ignores the student's submitted steps and solves from scratch without referencing their work
- Gives contradictory feedback

Score **0.5–0.8** for partial feedback (correct diagnosis but vague step reference, or good diagnosis but immediately gives full answer)

## Instructions
Think step by step.
